Web Site Critiques



Endoftheworld2012

http://www.endoftheworld2012.net/
  1. My first impression of this site was that there was a lot of information presented in overloaded kind of way. The main page was long, so immediately it was hard to take everything in all at once, with mostly being words on the main page.

  2. The site does not organize information very well. Most of the information is presented in a cluttered manner, making it difficult to find specific details.

  3. The sites structure is confusing and does not provide a clear path for users to navigate.

  4. The graphics / layout does not enhance the site and instead detracts from it.

  5. The load time of the site was reasonable. Which for a site with little media content is acceptable.

  6. The sites text size and color were adequate for readability.

  7. The site has small navigation bar at the top of the page. But once you scroll down, it becomes difficult to access.

  8. The site lacks a consistent design theme across all pages.

  9. The site responded well to smaller windows sizes and maintained its functionality.

  10. The site didnt offer anything particularly unique or well done. It was more reminisent of a older style webpage from the late 90s aside from a few embedded videos.
